## The honest read

Holochain has zero hApps with hundreds of thousands of users. The Volla phone preload is the closest thing to consumer distribution and that's a niche European hardware OEM partnership.

The serious users are framework developers (hREA, Neighbourhoods) building tooling for other framework developers. This is the "developer-tools-for-developer-tools" trap: good infrastructure that doesn't surface as end-user products.

## Implications for Myrhiza

- **Plan for the consumer-distribution gap.** Holochain has had a working framework for years and still no flagship consumer app. Whatever the runtime quality, getting to a flagship app is a separate, large problem.
- **Builder-tools-for-builders ecosystems can stall.** Make sure Myrhiza's own app demos are aimed at end users, not just at other framework developers.
- **OEM/hardware partnerships matter for distribution.** Holochain's Volla deal is real product reach. P2P runtimes don't get app-store discovery; preinstalled-on-device is one of the few channels that works.
